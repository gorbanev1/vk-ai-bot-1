Gigorave V170 — automatic VK history backfill

- Added startup history recovery for the recreated VK chat.
- Long Poll remains responsible for live messages; startup recovery fills messages that existed before the bot was online or were missed while it was offline.
- First successful recovery is a full pass; later runs are incremental with overlap.
- Recovered incoming messages are inserted into the same canonical peer history without overwriting the already linked predecessor-chat history.
- Recovery state is durable so restarts do not blindly repeat completed work.
