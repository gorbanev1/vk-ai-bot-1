# V188.26 — six-hour verified SQLite backups

- Added a runtime healthy-backup scheduler. A bot that stays online now creates a healthy bot.sqlite snapshot every 6 hours; backups are no longer startup-only.
- Retention increased from 5 to 15 healthy SQLite snapshots.
- Backup rotation accepts both legacy `bot-startup-*.sqlite` and new `bot-healthy-*.sqlite` files.
- New snapshots are created with SQLite `VACUUM INTO`, checked using `PRAGMA quick_check`, and only then admitted into rotation. A failed new snapshot does not cause deletion of an older good backup.
- Startup preflight still creates a fresh snapshot if the latest healthy backup is at least 6 hours old.
- Periodic failures retry after 10 minutes and do not replace a known-good backup.
- `RECOVER_BOT_DB.mjs` now recognizes both legacy and new healthy-backup filenames.
- Release remains clean: no `data/`, SQLite, WAL/SHM, or runtime journals are packaged.
